    How to play

    Ride the elevator with up to 4 friends. Every stop is a random floor with a job to do. Finish it, then get back in the elevator before the doors close. Everyone who makes it back earns that floor's hat. Fail a floor and the elevator loses a heart. Lose all 3 and the run is over.

    Your stamina bar drains when you sprint, jump, climb or carry heavy things. It refills when you rest on the ground. Getting hurt eats into it in red, and the elevator heals you. If the red fills up, you're knocked out and become a ghost until the next floor.
