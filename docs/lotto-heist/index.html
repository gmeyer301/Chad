Sneak free scratch tickets past the staff and cameras at Quik Stop with up to 4 friends. Go home every night, scratch your tickets, and make the quota or the run ends.

Hat
Game idea: s0meTh1ng.MP4 / Henry & Claude.AI

How to play

Move: WASD or arrow keys (joystick on phones).
Hold E at the lotto stand to take a ticket. Your first ticket each week is allowed. Every ticket after that is stealing, so don't let a worker or camera see you take it.
If you're seen, a worker chases you. Break line of sight, or swap hats at the hat rack to lose them. If they catch you, you lose the tickets in your pocket and you're banned from the store for 15 seconds.
Walk to the van in the parking lot to stash tickets so they're safe.
E at a can display knocks it over, and nearby staff go to check on it. Q honks and pulls staff toward you, which is handy for helping a friend or for trolling one.
The clerk sometimes gets on the phone. That's your window.
When the day ends, everyone goes home to scratch their tickets. If the team is short of the quota, the run is over.
