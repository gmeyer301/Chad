        THE JOB

        • Find blue scolite crystals. Aim your Extractor and click: CAPLOW.
        • Shards go into your harvester. Walk into the elevator to deposit them.
        • Meet the quota and the elevator leaves in 60 seconds. Entities get faster. Anyone outside gets left behind.
        • Level 0 → Poolrooms → Level Fun → The School → Level 5 → ...?

        CONTROLS

        • WASD move, Shift run, mouse to look
        • Click use item, E grab or interact
        • 1-5 or wheel: items, Q drop, F flashlight
        • V third person, Tab crew, Esc pause
        • Phone: left side moves, right side looks, buttons for the rest.

        ITEMS

        • Almond Water: +40 sanity
        • Glorp's Energy: speed boost
        • Moth Jelly: slows and poisons you
        • Flashlight: see in the dark (F)
        • Memory Jar: throw at a fallen teammate to revive them
        • Cuddles Candy: +35 health
        • Health Rock: hold 3s to eat, hold 5s on a teammate to feed them

        TALKING

        • Press T or Enter (or TALK on phones) to say something.
        • Whoever you're looking at or standing near answers. Bots far away answer over the walkie-talkie.
        • Start with @Name to pick who hears you. Press E on a bot to talk to it.
        • Bots, Cuddles, Party Poopers, the Boss and some spooky entities all talk back. In multiplayer, everyone sees the chat.

        SURVIVAL

        • Sanity drains in the backrooms, faster near entities and in the dark. At zero, you see the eye.
        • Cuddles is friendly. Pet Cuddles. Party Poopers sometimes hand out gifts.
        • Don't fall in the deep end of the Poolrooms.
        • Play the arcade at HQ for tokens and spend them on upgrades.
